Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Overview

VA Law is an educational website for students, families, and educators exploring legal careers. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

Information You May Voluntarily Submit

If you use our contact form or newsletter form, you may choose to submit information such as your name, email address, and message content. Those submissions are processed by our third-party form provider, Formspree, so they can be delivered to us.

Analytics and Cookies

At this time, VA Law does not use advertising cookies or behavior-based tracking on the site. If analytics or additional third-party tools are added later, this policy should be updated to explain what is collected, how it is used, and whether cookies are set.

Third-Party Links

Our site links to outside organizations, schools, and resources. We are not responsible for the privacy or security practices of those third-party websites, and we encourage you to review their policies before sharing personal information.

Email and Contact Safety

We recommend that visitors avoid sending sensitive personal, financial, or legal information through the website forms. Content submitted through forms may be stored by the form service provider in order to deliver messages and prevent abuse.

Children and Educational Use

Because this site may be used by students, we encourage parents, guardians, and educators to review online safety practices with younger users. VA Law is intended for educational guidance only and does not provide legal advice.

Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated as the site changes. If major features such as analytics, newsletters, or new integrations are added, the policy should be revised to reflect those practices.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, please visit the contact page.
