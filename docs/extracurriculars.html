Extracurricular Activities

Develop skills and experience outside the classroom that will strengthen your law school applications and build your confidence.

Recommended Activities

Debate Club

Participating in debate helps you research complex topics, form persuasive arguments and think on your feet — all essential skills for law students.

Mock Trial

Join a mock trial team to simulate courtroom proceedings. You’ll learn about courtroom etiquette, legal reasoning and team collaboration.

Student Government

Running for student government or serving on committees fosters leadership abilities and gives insight into policy‑making and governance.

Community Service

Volunteer with local nonprofits or legal aid organizations. Community service demonstrates commitment to public service and exposes you to social issues that lawyers often address.

Journalism & Writing

Writing for the school newspaper or creating a blog sharpens your writing skills and teaches you how to communicate complex ideas clearly.

Leadership Programs

Attend youth leadership programs or participate in clubs like Future Business Leaders of America (FBLA) and DECA to enhance public speaking and organizational skills.
