Helpful Resources

Explore tools, organizations and programs that can support you on your path to law school and beyond. Use the filters to narrow your search.

Filter by Category

Virginia State Bar

The official organization regulating attorneys in Virginia. Visit the Virginia State Bar to learn about legal education requirements, bar exam information and public resources.

American Bar Association

The American Bar Association provides extensive guidance on law schools, career paths and scholarships. Their website offers valuable resources for prospective law students.

LSAC & LSAT Preparation

Find information about the LSAT and law school admissions through the Law School Admission Council (LSAC). The LSAC website offers free practice tests and application support.

Internships & Shadowing

Reach out to local law firms, courts or legal aid organizations to arrange internships or job shadowing. Hands‑on experience helps you understand daily legal work and build professional connections.

Virginia Mock Trial & Debate

Participate in state and national programs like the Virginia High School Mock Trial League or the Virginia Debate Association to gain courtroom experience and sharpen your advocacy skills.

Pre‑Law Summer Programs

Many colleges, including UVA and William & Mary, offer summer institutes or enrichment programs for high school students interested in law. Check university websites for the latest offerings.

Scholarships & Financial Aid

Research local and national scholarships specifically for future law students. Start with your school’s guidance office and the VSB Pro Bono Scholarship directory.
