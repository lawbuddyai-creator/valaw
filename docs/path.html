Path to Becoming a Lawyer

Understand the milestones from high school through law school and beyond.

Steps to Law

High School Preparation

Focus on strong academics, especially in English, history, government, and speech. Participate in debate or mock trial to build critical thinking and public speaking skills.

Undergraduate Degree

Attend a four‑year college and choose a major that develops analytical and writing skills (such as political science, history, English or philosophy). Maintain a strong GPA and build relationships with professors for future recommendations.

Law School (J.D.)

Take the LSAT and apply to accredited law schools. Law school typically lasts three years and covers foundational legal topics such as contracts, torts and constitutional law. Virginia’s top schools include UVA Law, William & Mary Law School, George Mason’s Scalia Law and the University of Richmond School of Law.

Bar Exam & Licensing

After graduating from law school, you must pass the Virginia Bar Exam and meet character and fitness requirements to practice law. Continuing legal education is required to maintain your license.

Preparing Early

Even in high school, you can set yourself up for success by reading widely, improving your writing skills, and exploring internships or shadowing opportunities at local law firms or courthouses.
