Recommended High School Classes

Build a strong academic foundation with courses that enhance your writing, analytical and critical thinking skills.

Suggested Coursework

English & Writing

Take honors or AP English courses to improve your reading comprehension and writing ability. Strong writing skills are essential for drafting legal documents and arguments.

U.S. History & Government

Classes like U.S. history, civics and AP Government help you understand how laws are made and how they impact society. A solid grasp of history and civics is critical for law students.

Public Speaking & Debate

Speech and debate classes teach you how to articulate your thoughts clearly and persuasively. These skills will serve you well in courtroom settings and client interactions.

Economics & Business

Understanding economics and business concepts can be beneficial, especially if you plan to practice corporate or business law. Consider taking AP Economics or entrepreneurship courses.

Foreign Language

Learning another language broadens your cultural understanding and can be advantageous in areas such as immigration law or international practice.

Advanced Placement (AP) Courses

AP courses like AP U.S. Government & Politics, AP U.S. History, AP Economics and AP English can prepare you for the rigor of college coursework and strengthen your college applications.
