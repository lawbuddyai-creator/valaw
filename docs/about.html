About VA Law

Helping Virginia students explore law, leadership, and service.

Our Mission and Focus

VA Law was created to provide clear, accessible guidance for high school students in Virginia who dream of becoming attorneys. Navigating the journey to law school can be complex, and our goal is to demystify the process by offering curated information, resources and inspiration.

Who We Are

We are a team of educators, law students and legal professionals who are passionate about mentorship and community outreach. We understand the unique challenges and opportunities facing Virginia students and strive to empower you with practical advice and up‑to‑date information. This project began as a volunteer initiative and continues to grow thanks to contributions from students and lawyers across the Commonwealth.

Get Involved

If you have suggestions, questions or would like to share your own story of pursuing law, please reach out via our contact page. We believe in the power of community and welcome contributions from students, parents, teachers and lawyers across Virginia.
