Outreach Email Templates

A respectful, clear email can open doors. Use these templates to reach out to legislators, attorneys, nonprofits, and internship programs. Keep it short, specific, and polite.

Suggest an improvement →

Before you send

  • Use a professional email address (your name if possible).
  • Keep it under ~200 words.
  • Say why you chose them (district, committee, practice area, program, etc.).
  • Ask for one clear thing: a short call, a few questions by email, or permission to attend an event.
  • Follow up once after 7–10 days if you hear nothing.

Templates

1) Delegate / Legislator

Best for: informational interview about public service

Subject: Virginia student hoping to learn about public service Hello Delegate [Last Name], My name is [Your Name], and I’m a high school student in [City], Virginia interested in law and public service. I’m reaching out because [one specific reason: committee you serve on / bill topic / local issue]. If you’re open to it, could I ask a few questions (by email or a brief call) about what your work is like and how students can start preparing? Thank you for your time and for serving our community. Sincerely, [Your Full Name] [School Name] • [City, VA]

2) Attorney / Law firm

Best for: informational interview or shadowing request

Subject: Student interested in learning about [Practice Area] Hello Mr./Ms. [Last Name], My name is [Your Name], and I’m a high school student in [City], Virginia interested in pursuing law. I found your work through [how: firm website / article / recommendation], and I’m especially interested in [one specific detail]. If you have 10–15 minutes, I’d appreciate the chance to ask a few questions about your career path and what students can do now to prepare. Thank you for considering my request. Best regards, [Your Full Name] [School Name] • [City, VA]

3) Nonprofit / Advocacy organization

Best for: volunteering or youth program inquiry

Subject: Student interested in supporting [Organization Name] Hello [Name/Team], My name is [Your Name], and I’m a high school student in [City], Virginia interested in [topic: civil rights / environment / community service]. I’m reaching out because I admire your work on [specific program or issue]. Are there any student-friendly ways to get involved (volunteering, events, youth programs, or educational materials)? Thank you for your time and for the work you do. Sincerely, [Your Full Name] [School Name] • [City, VA]

4) Internship / Summer program

Best for: programs & internship questions

Subject: Question about [Program/Internship Name] (Virginia student) Hello [Program Coordinator/Name], My name is [Your Name], and I’m a high school student in [City], Virginia interested in law and public service. I’m considering applying to [Program/Internship Name]. Could you share the best way to learn about eligibility, deadlines, and what strong applicants typically have? If there’s an info session or webpage you recommend, I’d love to review it. Thank you for your help. Best regards, [Your Full Name] [School Name] • [City, VA]

5) Follow-up (7–10 days later)

Best for: one polite follow-up

Subject: Follow-up — student request Hello [Title] [Last Name], I’m following up on my email from [date]. If you’re able to share any guidance or have a brief moment for a few questions, I’d really appreciate it. Thank you again for your time. Sincerely, [Your Full Name]
