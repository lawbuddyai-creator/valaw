Outreach Email Templates

Want to learn from real professionals? A respectful, clear email can open doors. Use these templates to reach out to delegates, legislators, attorneys, judges, and organizations.

Quick rule: Keep it short, specific, and polite. Proofread before you send.

Before you send

  • Use a professional email address (your name if possible).
  • Keep it under ~200 words.
  • Say why you chose them (location, committee, practice area, casework, etc.).
  • Ask for one clear thing: a short call, a few questions by email, or permission to attend an event.
  • If you attach a resume, mention it in one line (and name the file clearly).

Template 1: Delegate / Legislator

Subject: Virginia student hoping to learn about public service

Hello Delegate [Last Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I’m a high school student in [City], Virginia interested in law and public service.

I’m reaching out because [one specific reason: committee you serve on / bill topic / local issue]. If you’re open to it, could I ask a few questions (by email or a brief call) about what your work is like and how students can start preparing?

Thank you for your time and for serving our community.

Sincerely,
[Your Full Name]
[School Name] • [City, VA]

Template 2: Attorney / Law firm (informational interview)

Subject: Student interested in learning about [Practice Area]

Hello Mr./Ms. [Last Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I’m a high school student in [City], Virginia interested in pursuing law.

I found your work through [how: firm website / article / recommendation], and I’m especially interested in [one specific detail]. If you have 10–15 minutes, I’d appreciate the chance to ask a few questions about your career path and what students can do now to prepare.

Thank you for considering my request.

Best regards,
[Your Full Name]
[School Name] • [City, VA]

Template 3: Judge / Court professional (extra formal)

Subject: Student inquiry about the court system

Dear Judge [Last Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I am a high school student in [City], Virginia with a strong interest in law and the judicial system.

If permitted, I would be grateful for any general guidance about how students can learn more about court operations or prepare for careers in law. I understand your schedule is very busy and appreciate any direction you can provide.

Respectfully,
[Your Full Name]
[School Name] • [City, VA]

Follow‑up (if you hear nothing after 7–10 days)

Subject: Follow‑up — student request

Hello [Title] [Last Name],

I’m following up on my email from [date]. If you’re able to share any guidance or have a brief moment for a few questions, I’d really appreciate it.

Thank you again for your time.

Sincerely,
[Your Full Name]
