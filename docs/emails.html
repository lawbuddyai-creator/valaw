Professional Outreach Email Templates

Reaching out professionally is an important skill for anyone interested in law, government, or public service. These templates are designed for students contacting legislators, attorneys, judges, and organizations.

Before You Send an Email

  • Use a professional email address (your name if possible).
  • Keep your message under 200 words.
  • Be specific about why you are reaching out.
  • Always be respectful and polite.

Template 1: Delegate or Legislator

Subject: Virginia Student Seeking to Learn About Public Service

Hello Delegate [Last Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I am a high school student in Virginia with an interest in law and public service.

I am reaching out to respectfully ask if you would be willing to share a bit about your work and how you became involved in government. I am eager to learn how students can begin preparing for careers in public service.

Thank you for your time and for serving our community.

Sincerely,
[Your Full Name]
[School Name]
[City, VA]

Template 2: Attorney or Law Firm

Subject: High School Student Interested in Learning About the Legal Profession

Hello Mr./Ms. [Last Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I am a high school student interested in pursuing a career in law.

I am writing to ask whether you might be willing to share insights about your work as an attorney. I would greatly appreciate the opportunity to learn from your experience.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
[Your Full Name]
[School Name]
[City, VA]

Template 3: Judge or Court Professional

Subject: Student Inquiry About the Legal System

Dear Judge [Last Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I am a high school student in Virginia with a strong interest in law and the judicial system.

I am writing to respectfully ask if you might be willing to offer general advice about careers in law or ways students can learn more about court operations.

Thank you very much for your time and service.

Respectfully,
[Your Full Name]
[School Name]
[City, VA]
