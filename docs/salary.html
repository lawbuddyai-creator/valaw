Lawyer Salaries & Career Outcomes

Law can be a rewarding career — financially and intellectually — but outcomes vary widely. This page breaks down realistic salary ranges, career paths, and cost considerations.

Starting Salaries (Approximate)

  • Public Defender / Legal Aid: $50k–$70k
  • Government Attorney: $60k–$90k
  • Small Firm / Local Practice: $55k–$80k
  • Mid-size Firm: $80k–$130k
  • Big Law (Large Firms): $190k–$215k (very competitive)

Salaries vary by location, school, grades, and market conditions.

Law-Adjacent Careers (No Law School Required)

  • Paralegal: $45k–$75k
  • Policy Analyst: $60k–$100k
  • Compliance Officer: $70k–$120k
  • Legislative Aide: $40k–$80k
  • Legal Tech Roles: $80k–$150k

Is Law School Worth It?

  • Do you enjoy reading, writing, and research?
  • Are you comfortable with delayed payoff?
  • Have you reviewed job outcomes?
