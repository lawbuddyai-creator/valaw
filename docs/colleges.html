Transfer & community college path

  • Save money: community college tuition is usually much lower.
  • Protect your GPA: build strong academic habits early.
  • Plan transfers: meet with an advisor so credits transfer cleanly.
  • Law school is later: you’ll still apply to law school after earning your bachelor’s degree.

What to research before choosing a school

  • Does the school have a pre‑law advisor or advising office?
  • Are there writing-intensive courses and strong professors for recommendations?
  • Internships nearby (courts, firms, nonprofits, government)?
  • Total cost: tuition, housing, scholarships, financial aid.
  • Programs you’ll actually use (mock trial, debate, research labs, honors).
  • Location: proximity to courthouses and legal employers.
  • Study abroad or honors opportunities if that fits your goals.
