Top Virginia Colleges & Law Schools

Explore Virginia’s universities and law schools that will prepare you for a successful legal career. Research academic offerings, pre‑law advising and campus culture to find your best fit.

Virginia Institutions

University of Virginia (UVA)

UVA offers a strong liberal arts education and is home to the prestigious University of Virginia School of Law in Charlottesville. Its programs emphasize practical skills, public service and academic excellence.

William & Mary

The College of William & Mary in Williamsburg has one of the nation’s oldest law schools. William & Mary Law School offers rigorous academics and numerous clinics that provide real‑world experience.

George Mason University

Located in Arlington, the Antonin Scalia Law School at George Mason University offers an innovative curriculum with a focus on the economic analysis of law and practical skills training.

University of Richmond

The University of Richmond School of Law offers a supportive community and abundant opportunities for clinical and experiential learning.

Pre‑Law Programs

Many Virginia colleges—including Virginia Tech, Virginia Commonwealth University (VCU) and James Madison University—offer pre‑law advising, majors like political science or philosophy, and opportunities to network with local attorneys.
